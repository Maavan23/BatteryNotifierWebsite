Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up Battery Notifier, configuring thresholds, and protecting lithium battery longevity.

General Questions

What is Battery Notifier?

Battery Notifier is a free, lightweight, privacy-focused Windows application designed to monitor laptop battery levels in the system tray and issue customizable audio and visual alerts when charging or discharging thresholds are reached.

What's new in latest release?

The latest release improves application runtime stability, optimizes asset path resolution for bundled resources (battery icon, alert sound chimes), ensures user data (settings.json and history.json) is stored safely in %APPDATA%\BatteryNotifier, and minimizes memory footprint under 12MB RAM.

Is it completely free? Are there any ads or telemetry?

Battery Notifier is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. It contains no advertisement modules, no background telemetry, and no telemetry analytics. It operates completely offline.

Installation & Troubleshooting

Where are my settings and notification logs stored?

User configuration files (settings.json) and historical alert logs (history.json) are persistently stored in the Windows user directory:
%APPDATA%\BatteryNotifier
This guarantees your custom threshold choices and audio settings survive app updates and re-installations.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit edition). No additional software runtimes are required; the installer includes all bundled dependencies.

How do I configure custom audio chime sounds?

Open Battery Notifier from your Windows notification tray, click on Settings & Audio, and choose Browse Sound File. Select any .mp3 or .wav sound file on your PC.
