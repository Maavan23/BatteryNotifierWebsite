Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about battery chemistry or software setup? We've got answers.

Lithium-ion battery cells experience exponentially higher chemical stress when charged above 4.10V/cell (approx 80% to 100% capacity). Capping charge at 80% reduces internal heat build-up and cathode degradation, expanding the battery’s overall cycle lifespan from ~300-500 cycles to over 1,500 cycles.
Battery Notifier is a lightweight notification utility that triggers audible and visual Windows notifications when thresholds are reached. Windows user-space software cannot directly cut physical AC wall power without manufacturer-specific hardware EC firmware drivers. Battery Notifier ensures you are immediately aware so you can unplug your laptop or connect power.
